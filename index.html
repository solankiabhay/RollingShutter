Shutter Problems We Fix: Spring, Motor, Lock, Greasing & More

Broken or Loose Shutter Spring

A broken spring makes the shutter heavy, uneven or impossible to lift. We replace it with a high-tensile spring and re-balance the shutter. Do not force it open, as a snapped spring can be dangerous.

Shutter Stuck or Jammed

Shutter not opening or not closing fully? Common causes are bent slats, damaged guide rails, a seized spring or a lock left engaged. We find the cause first, then repair.

Shutter Off Track or Guide Rail Damage

If the shutter jumps out of the channel or scrapes the side, we straighten or replace the guide rails and re-align the slats.

Shutter Motor Not Working

Dead motor, humming sound or a shutter that stops halfway. We repair or replace side and tubular motors, limit switches and control panels.

Remote or Receiver Not Working

Remote not responding, short range or random opening. We re-sync remotes, replace batteries and receivers, and fix wall switches.

Noisy or Heavy Shutter

Screeching, rattling or a shutter that is hard to pull usually needs greasing, roller replacement or spring adjustment.

Shutter Greasing and Oiling

Regular greasing of guide rails, rollers and pivots keeps the shutter smooth and extends its life. Available as a one-time visit or under AMC.

Lock, Key and Handle Problems

Broken lock, lost key, jammed central lock or loose handle. We repair or replace shutter locks and handles.

Bent or Damaged Slats (Lathi)

Bent, dented or broken slats after a vehicle hit or forced opening are straightened, welded or replaced, including bottom rail work.

Gearbox, Chain and Sprocket Faults

Gear shutters that slip, grind or jam need a gearbox overhaul or chain and sprocket replacement.

Rapid Roll and Fire-Rated Door Faults

Sensor, photocell, auto-close and fusible link problems on industrial rapid roll doors and fire-rated shutters.

New Shutter or Replacement

When repair costs more than it is worth, we install a new manual, gear or motorized shutter in a custom size for your shop, godown or factory.

Why is my rolling shutter making noise, and does greasing help?

Noise usually comes from dry guide rails, worn rollers or pivots, or a loose spring or chain. Greasing the guide channels, rollers and pivot points often reduces the noise. If the noise continues, spring tension, bent slats or worn gearbox parts may need repair. Send a short video on WhatsApp at 9309391112 and we will tell you the likely cause.

How often should a rolling shutter be greased or serviced?

Shops and warehouses that open and close the shutter every day usually benefit from servicing every 3 to 6 months. Lightly used shutters should be checked at least once a year. An AMC covers scheduled greasing and inspection visits.

My motorized shutter is not working. What should I check first?

Check the power supply, the fuse, the remote battery, and whether the manual override or chain has been left engaged. If the shutter still does not respond, the motor, capacitor, limit switch or receiver may be faulty. Do not force the shutter; call us at 9309391112.

Can a broken shutter spring be replaced on the same day?

In most cases we can inspect and replace a broken spring on the same day, depending on the shutter size and spring availability. Avoid forcing a shutter with a broken spring, as it can be dangerous.

Do you repair shutter locks and replace lost keys?

Yes. We repair and replace shutter locks, central locks and handles, and fix lock problems caused by bent slats or misaligned guide rails.

Do you install new rolling shutters in Pune?
